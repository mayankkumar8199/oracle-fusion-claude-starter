## 2. Procure-to-Pay (P2P) end to end

This section follows the 26D Procure-to-Pay cycle step by step. Each step has the same compact table: purpose, roles, where it lives in the UI, tables, statuses, REST roots, bulk and scheduled processes, and the controls that usually cause exceptions. Table names, primary keys, column names and column comments come from the 26D Tables and Views guides. UI paths, roles and process behaviour come from the 26D Using and Implementing guides, the security references and the 26D readiness notes. Anything not confirmed there is marked "(verify in your pod)". Full REST paths are in section 6. FBDI templates and scheduled-process parameters are in section 7. Subledger accounting internals are in section 4. The Payables Agent and Payments Agent are in section 10.

### P2P flow at a glance

| Step | Business event | Primary document and table | Accounting impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 Supplier onboarding | Registration approved; supplier, address, site, site assignment, contact and bank account created | Supplier: POZ_SUPPLIERS, POZ_SUPPLIER_SITES_ALL_M, POZ_SITE_ASSIGNMENTS_ALL_M; party HZ_PARTIES; payee IBY_EXTERNAL_PAYEES_ALL | None. The site assignment holds the default liability and prepayment accounts and the distribution set |
| 2.2 Requisitioning | Requisition submitted and approved | POR_REQUISITION_HEADERS_ALL, POR_REQUISITION_LINES_ALL, POR_REQ_DISTRIBUTIONS_ALL | No GL journal. With Budgetary Control, funds are reserved at submission or at approval. Encumbrance accounting is created only when the requisition is implemented |
| 2.3 Sourcing and agreements (optional) | Negotiation awarded; blanket or contract agreement approved | PON_AUCTION_HEADERS_ALL, PON_BID_HEADERS; agreement in PO_HEADERS_ALL (TYPE_LOOKUP_CODE BLANKET or CONTRACT) | None |
| 2.4 Purchase orders | PO approved, communicated, acknowledged; change orders processed | PO_HEADERS_ALL, PO_LINES_ALL, PO_LINE_LOCATIONS_ALL, PO_DISTRIBUTIONS_ALL | None, unless Budgetary Control or encumbrance is used (FUNDS_STATUS at every level) |
| 2.5 Receiving | Receive, inspect, deliver or put away, correct, return; ASN | RCV_SHIPMENT_HEADERS, RCV_SHIPMENT_LINES, RCV_TRANSACTIONS | Accrue at receipt: Receipt Accounting records the accrual when the item is received (CMR_RCV_DISTRIBUTIONS). Accrue at period end: uninvoiced receipt accrual at period end, reversed in the next period by Create Accrual Reversal Accounting |
| 2.6 Invoice capture | Invoice entered, imported, scanned or submitted by the supplier | AP_INVOICES_ALL, AP_INVOICE_LINES_ALL | None until the invoice is validated and accounted |
| 2.7 Matching, validation, holds, approval | Invoice matched and validated; holds applied or released; invoice approved | AP_INVOICE_DISTRIBUTIONS_ALL, AP_HOLDS_ALL | Event Invoice Validated. For accrue-at-receipt POs the receipt accrual is reversed and the liability is recorded. Price and conversion-rate variances go to variance accounts |
| 2.8 Prepayments, memos, withholding | Prepayment applied; credit or debit memo validated; withholding calculated | AP_INVOICES_ALL (invoice type), AP_INVOICE_LINES_ALL (prepayment and withholding lines), ZX_WITHHOLDING_LINES | Events Prepayment Applied or Unapplied, Credit Memo Validated, Debit Memo Validated; withholding invoice to the tax authority |
| 2.9 Payments | Payment process request completed; quick, manual or refund payment; void or stop | AP_CHECKS_ALL, AP_INVOICE_PAYMENTS_ALL, IBY_PAYMENTS_ALL, IBY_PAY_INSTRUCTIONS_ALL | Event Payment Created. "At payment issue": debit liability, credit cash. "At payment issue and clearing": credit cash clearing. "At payment clearing": nothing until clearing |
| 2.10 Accounting | Create Accounting run; journals transferred and posted | XLA_EVENTS, XLA_AE_HEADERS, XLA_AE_LINES; GL_JE_HEADERS, GL_JE_LINES | Subledger journals in the ledger |
| 2.11 Bank reconciliation | Statement line reconciled; payment cleared | CE_STATEMENT_HEADERS, CE_STATEMENT_LINES, CE_RECON_HISTORY_ITEMS; AP_PAYMENT_HISTORY_ALL | Event Payment Cleared. "At payment clearing": debit liability, credit cash. "At payment issue and clearing": debit cash clearing, credit cash |
| 2.12 Period close | Payables period closed; receipt accruals booked; reconciliations signed off; GL period closed | GL_PERIOD_STATUSES, CMR_PERIOD_STATUSES, CMR_ACCRUAL_SUMMARY | Uninvoiced receipt accruals; unaccounted transactions swept to the next period |

The accounting column follows the Payables variance-account rules, the payment accounting option and the uninvoiced receipt accrual process ([Variance Accounts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/financials/26d/fappp/variance-accounts.html), [Guidelines for Payment Accounting Options](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/financials/26d/faipp/guidelines-for-payment-accounting-options.html), [Create Uninvoiced Receipt Accrual](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/supply-chain-and-manufacturing/26d/faspc/create-uninvoiced-receipt-accrual.html)). Oracle says not to change the payment accounting option after the first implementation.

The query below traces one purchase order through the whole spine. It is a diagnostic query for one PO, not a report. The CTEs aggregate receipts, holds, payments and accounting so the grain stays at one row per PO distribution and matched invoice distribution. It uses Oracle Database 19c syntax (LISTAGG DISTINCT). Bind `:payables_application_id` to the Payables application ID (200 in published Fusion queries; confirm with the XLA_SUBLEDGERS query in 4.1.2 or helper H1 in 9.1).

```sql
WITH po AS (
  SELECT pda.PO_DISTRIBUTION_ID, pda.LINE_LOCATION_ID
  FROM   PO_HEADERS_ALL ph
  JOIN   PO_DISTRIBUTIONS_ALL pda ON pda.PO_HEADER_ID = ph.PO_HEADER_ID
  WHERE  ph.SEGMENT1 = :po_number
  AND    ph.PRC_BU_ID = :prc_bu_id
),
inv AS (
  SELECT DISTINCT aid.INVOICE_ID
  FROM   AP_INVOICE_DISTRIBUTIONS_ALL aid
  WHERE  aid.PO_DISTRIBUTION_ID IN (SELECT po.PO_DISTRIBUTION_ID FROM po)
),
rcv AS (
  SELECT rt.PO_LINE_LOCATION_ID,
         LISTAGG(DISTINCT rsh.RECEIPT_NUM, ', ') WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY rsh.RECEIPT_NUM) AS receipt_numbers,
         COUNT(*) AS receiving_txns,
         SUM(CASE WHEN rt.ACCRUAL_STATUS_CODE = 'INTERFACED' THEN 0 ELSE 1 END) AS txns_not_in_costing,
         MAX(rsl.SHIPMENT_LINE_STATUS_CODE) AS shipment_line_status
  FROM   RCV_TRANSACTIONS rt
  JOIN   RCV_SHIPMENT_LINES rsl   ON rsl.SHIPMENT_LINE_ID   = rt.SHIPMENT_LINE_ID
  JOIN   RCV_SHIPMENT_HEADERS rsh ON rsh.SHIPMENT_HEADER_ID = rsl.SHIPMENT_HEADER_ID
  WHERE  rt.PO_LINE_LOCATION_ID IN (SELECT po.LINE_LOCATION_ID FROM po)
  GROUP  BY rt.PO_LINE_LOCATION_ID
),
hld AS (
  SELECT ah.INVOICE_ID,
         LISTAGG(DISTINCT ah.HOLD_LOOKUP_CODE, ', ') WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY ah.HOLD_LOOKUP_CODE) AS open_holds
  FROM   AP_HOLDS_ALL ah
  WHERE  ah.RELEASE_LOOKUP_CODE IS NULL
  AND    ah.INVOICE_ID IN (SELECT inv.INVOICE_ID FROM inv)
  GROUP  BY ah.INVOICE_ID
),
pay AS (
  SELECT aps.INVOICE_ID,
         MAX(aps.HOLD_FLAG) AS installment_hold,
         MIN(aps.DUE_DATE)  AS first_due_date,
         LISTAGG(DISTINCT TO_CHAR(ac.CHECK_NUMBER), ', ') WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY TO_CHAR(ac.CHECK_NUMBER)) AS payment_numbers,
         MAX(ac.STATUS_LOOKUP_CODE)              AS payables_payment_status,
         MAX(ac.CLEARED_DATE)                    AS cleared_date,
         MAX(ipa.PAYMENT_STATUS)                 AS payments_payment_status,
         MAX(ipi.PAYMENT_INSTRUCTION_STATUS)     AS payment_file_status,
         MAX(ipr.CALL_APP_PAY_SERVICE_REQ_CODE)  AS ppr_name,
         COUNT(DISTINCT idp.DOCUMENT_PAYABLE_ID) AS documents_in_payments
  FROM   AP_PAYMENT_SCHEDULES_ALL aps
  LEFT JOIN AP_INVOICE_PAYMENTS_ALL aip  ON aip.INVOICE_ID = aps.INVOICE_ID AND aip.PAYMENT_NUM = aps.PAYMENT_NUM
  LEFT JOIN AP_CHECKS_ALL ac             ON ac.CHECK_ID = aip.CHECK_ID
  LEFT JOIN IBY_PAYMENTS_ALL ipa         ON ipa.PAYMENT_ID = ac.PAYMENT_ID
  LEFT JOIN IBY_DOCS_PAYABLE_ALL idp     ON idp.PAYMENT_ID = ipa.PAYMENT_ID
  LEFT JOIN IBY_PAY_INSTRUCTIONS_ALL ipi ON ipi.PAYMENT_INSTRUCTION_ID = ipa.PAYMENT_INSTRUCTION_ID
  LEFT JOIN IBY_PAY_SERVICE_REQUESTS ipr ON ipr.PAYMENT_SERVICE_REQUEST_ID = ipa.PAYMENT_SERVICE_REQUEST_ID
  WHERE  aps.INVOICE_ID IN (SELECT inv.INVOICE_ID FROM inv)
  GROUP  BY aps.INVOICE_ID
),
acct AS (
  SELECT d.INVOICE_DISTRIBUTION_ID,
         MAX(xe.EVENT_STATUS_CODE)             AS event_status,
         MAX(xte.TRANSACTION_NUMBER)           AS sla_transaction_number,
         MAX(xah.ACCOUNTING_ENTRY_STATUS_CODE) AS sla_entry_status,
         MAX(xah.GL_TRANSFER_STATUS_CODE)      AS transfer_to_gl_status,
         MAX(gjh.NAME)                         AS journal_name
  FROM   AP_INVOICE_DISTRIBUTIONS_ALL d
  JOIN   XLA_EVENTS xe                ON xe.EVENT_ID = d.ACCOUNTING_EVENT_ID
                                     AND xe.APPLICATION_ID = :payables_application_id
  JOIN   XLA_TRANSACTION_ENTITIES xte ON xte.ENTITY_ID = xe.ENTITY_ID AND xte.APPLICATION_ID = xe.APPLICATION_ID
  JOIN   XLA_DISTRIBUTION_LINKS xdl   ON xdl.EVENT_ID = xe.EVENT_ID AND xdl.APPLICATION_ID = xe.APPLICATION_ID
                                     AND xdl.SOURCE_DISTRIBUTION_ID_NUM_1 = d.INVOICE_DISTRIBUTION_ID
  JOIN   XLA_AE_LINES xal             ON xal.AE_HEADER_ID = xdl.AE_HEADER_ID AND xal.AE_LINE_NUM = xdl.AE_LINE_NUM
                                     AND xal.APPLICATION_ID = xdl.APPLICATION_ID
  JOIN   XLA_AE_HEADERS xah           ON xah.AE_HEADER_ID = xal.AE_HEADER_ID AND xah.APPLICATION_ID = xal.APPLICATION_ID
  LEFT JOIN GL_IMPORT_REFERENCES gir  ON gir.GL_SL_LINK_ID = xal.GL_SL_LINK_ID AND gir.GL_SL_LINK_TABLE = xal.GL_SL_LINK_TABLE
  LEFT JOIN GL_JE_LINES gjl           ON gjl.JE_HEADER_ID = gir.JE_HEADER_ID AND gjl.JE_LINE_NUM = gir.JE_LINE_NUM
  LEFT JOIN GL_JE_HEADERS gjh         ON gjh.JE_HEADER_ID = gjl.JE_HEADER_ID
  WHERE  d.PO_DISTRIBUTION_ID IN (SELECT po.PO_DISTRIBUTION_ID FROM po)
  GROUP  BY d.INVOICE_DISTRIBUTION_ID
)
SELECT rqh.REQUISITION_NUMBER,
       rql.LINE_NUMBER             AS req_line,
       rql.LINE_STATUS             AS req_line_status,
       ph.SEGMENT1                 AS po_number,
       ph.TYPE_LOOKUP_CODE         AS document_type,
       ph.DOCUMENT_STATUS          AS po_status,
       hp.PARTY_NAME               AS supplier_name,
       sup.SEGMENT1                AS supplier_number,
       sit.VENDOR_SITE_CODE        AS supplier_site,
       pl.LINE_NUM                 AS order_line,
       pll.SHIPMENT_NUM            AS schedule_num,
       pda.DISTRIBUTION_NUM        AS order_distribution,
       pll.MATCH_OPTION,
       pll.RECEIPT_REQUIRED_FLAG,
       pll.INSPECTION_REQUIRED_FLAG,
       pda.ACCRUE_ON_RECEIPT_FLAG,
       pll.QUANTITY                AS schedule_qty,
       pll.QUANTITY_RECEIVED,
       pll.QUANTITY_BILLED,
       rcv.receipt_numbers,
       rcv.txns_not_in_costing,
       ai.INVOICE_NUM,
       ai.INVOICE_TYPE_LOOKUP_CODE AS invoice_type,
       ai.WFAPPROVAL_STATUS        AS invoice_approval,
       ai.PAYMENT_STATUS_FLAG      AS invoice_paid_flag,
       ail.LINE_NUMBER             AS invoice_line,
       ail.MATCH_TYPE,
       aid.LINE_TYPE_LOOKUP_CODE   AS dist_type,
       aid.AMOUNT                  AS dist_amount,
       aid.MATCH_STATUS_FLAG,
       aid.POSTED_FLAG,
       rtm.TRANSACTION_ID          AS matched_receipt_txn,
       hld.open_holds,
       (SELECT SUM(s.AMOUNT_REMAINING) FROM AP_PAYMENT_SCHEDULES_ALL s WHERE s.INVOICE_ID = ai.INVOICE_ID) AS amount_remaining,
       pay.installment_hold,
       pay.payment_numbers,
       pay.payables_payment_status,
       pay.payments_payment_status,
       pay.payment_file_status,
       pay.ppr_name,
       pay.cleared_date,
       acct.sla_entry_status,
       acct.transfer_to_gl_status,
       acct.journal_name
FROM   PO_HEADERS_ALL ph
JOIN   POZ_SUPPLIERS sup                   ON sup.VENDOR_ID = ph.VENDOR_ID
JOIN   HZ_PARTIES hp                       ON hp.PARTY_ID = sup.PARTY_ID
LEFT JOIN POZ_SUPPLIER_SITES_ALL_M sit     ON sit.VENDOR_SITE_ID = ph.VENDOR_SITE_ID
JOIN   PO_LINES_ALL pl                     ON pl.PO_HEADER_ID = ph.PO_HEADER_ID
JOIN   PO_LINE_LOCATIONS_ALL pll           ON pll.PO_LINE_ID = pl.PO_LINE_ID
JOIN   PO_DISTRIBUTIONS_ALL pda            ON pda.LINE_LOCATION_ID = pll.LINE_LOCATION_ID
LEFT JOIN POR_REQ_DISTRIBUTIONS_ALL rqd    ON rqd.DISTRIBUTION_ID = pda.REQ_DISTRIBUTION_ID
LEFT JOIN POR_REQUISITION_LINES_ALL rql    ON rql.REQUISITION_LINE_ID = rqd.REQUISITION_LINE_ID
LEFT JOIN POR_REQUISITION_HEADERS_ALL rqh  ON rqh.REQUISITION_HEADER_ID = rql.REQUISITION_HEADER_ID
LEFT JOIN rcv                              ON rcv.PO_LINE_LOCATION_ID = pll.LINE_LOCATION_ID
LEFT JOIN AP_INVOICE_DISTRIBUTIONS_ALL aid ON aid.PO_DISTRIBUTION_ID = pda.PO_DISTRIBUTION_ID
LEFT JOIN AP_INVOICE_LINES_ALL ail         ON ail.INVOICE_ID = aid.INVOICE_ID AND ail.LINE_NUMBER = aid.INVOICE_LINE_NUMBER
LEFT JOIN AP_INVOICES_ALL ai               ON ai.INVOICE_ID = aid.INVOICE_ID
LEFT JOIN RCV_TRANSACTIONS rtm             ON rtm.TRANSACTION_ID = aid.RCV_TRANSACTION_ID
LEFT JOIN hld                              ON hld.INVOICE_ID = ai.INVOICE_ID
LEFT JOIN pay                              ON pay.INVOICE_ID = ai.INVOICE_ID
LEFT JOIN acct                             ON acct.INVOICE_DISTRIBUTION_ID = aid.INVOICE_DISTRIBUTION_ID
WHERE  ph.SEGMENT1 = :po_number
AND    ph.PRC_BU_ID = :prc_bu_id
ORDER  BY pl.LINE_NUM, pll.SHIPMENT_NUM, pda.DISTRIBUTION_NUM, ai.INVOICE_NUM, ail.LINE_NUMBER;
```

Notes on the query:

- Treating `RELEASE_LOOKUP_CODE IS NULL` as "unreleased" follows the column comment ("Release name that released the hold"). The table description warns that the same hold type can be placed, released and placed again on an invoice; the documented primary key is HOLD_ID.
- Receipts are aggregated per schedule (PO_LINE_LOCATION_ID). Standard and inspection receipts are entered at schedule level, so their receiving transactions may carry no PO_DISTRIBUTION_ID.
- Distribution-level accounting goes through XLA_DISTRIBUTION_LINKS. Header-level questions, such as "is this invoice fully accounted", are answered faster from POSTED_FLAG. More SQL is in section 9.
- This query is the wide diagnostic view (one row per PO distribution and matched invoice distribution). For a timeline with one row per document, use section 9, Q1; for the invoice-to-GL drill-down alone, use Q7.
